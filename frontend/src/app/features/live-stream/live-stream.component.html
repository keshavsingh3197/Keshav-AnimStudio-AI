Does the stream key change?

No - not on its own. YouTube's default stream key is reusable and stays the same until you press Reset in YouTube Studio, or create another key. That's why it can be saved once per channel. If it's ever reset, the next stream is refused, the channel is flagged here, and you're asked to save the new key. Turn on auto-start and auto-stop in the stream settings so the broadcast starts and ends with the stream.

Good to know

  • Sending a prepared stream copies files instead of encoding live, so a 24/7 loop barely touches the CPU.
  • A prepared stream can be sent again - after it ends, or after a drop - without preparing it again.
  • YouTube takes 10–30 seconds to show a new stream as live.
  • With Dual stream on, YouTube also crops a vertical feed for Shorts from a landscape stream.
  • Prepared streams are kept for a few hours, and are cleared when the server restarts.
