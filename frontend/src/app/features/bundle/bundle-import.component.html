Bundle

Build a whole video from one file. A bundle is a .zip holding the sheets that describe your project and a media/ folder with your images and audio. No AI provider, key or internet connection is needed for any of this.

1. Start from the template

The template has one example row per sheet and an empty media/ folder. Open the .csv files in Excel, Numbers, LibreOffice or Google Sheets.

The prompt pack is for filling the sheets in with any free AI chat you already use — the AI part happens outside this application, so nothing needs configuring here. Anything you paste into an outside tool leaves your machine, so fill the sheets in by hand if the material is private.

2. Upload it back

Put your images and audio in media/, refer to them from the sheets by their filename, zip the folder, and drop it here. You will see every change before anything is saved.
