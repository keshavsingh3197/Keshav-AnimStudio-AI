Build your effects kit

Anything you put on top of videos again and again belongs here. A few ideas:

  • 🔔 Subscribe & like - a button animation on a transparent (WebM/MOV) or green background
  • ⚡ Transitions - a whoosh stinger between two scenes
  • 🏁 End screens - your outro card; "Add at end" lines it up with the last second
  • 🏷 Lower thirds - a name bar or a PNG title strip
  • 🔊 Sound effects - whoosh, ding, pop, camera shutter
  • 🟩 Green screen - reaction or meme clips; the green is keyed out in the export

Already have one in a project? Use Save to effects on it in the editor's media panel.
