Tips for a natural, human-sounding voice
  • Write the way it should be said: commas and full stops become breaths and pauses.
  • Speed 0.9–0.95× reads as calm narration; 1.0× and above starts to sound rushed.
  • Hindi script (देवनागरी) needs a Hindi voice; the panel switches to one automatically.
  • Keep lines to one or two sentences so each fits its clip, and nudge any line by dragging it on A1.
  • If your clips have their own sound, set the Mix overlap rule to Duck video so the voice leads.
