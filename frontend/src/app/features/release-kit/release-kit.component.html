🎵 Music Release Kit

Turn a finished song into everything a distributor asks for: a loudness-normalised WAV master, 3000×3000 cover art, a release sheet to copy from, and promo videos for YouTube, Reels and Spotify Canvas.

How this reaches Spotify, Apple Music and other stores: stores only take uploads from distributors they have contracts with. This kit prepares your files so the upload to your distributor is quick and doesn't get rejected. You can upload the visualizer video to YouTube yourself.

Next steps

  1. Pick a distributor and compare its fees, royalty split and payout terms. These change often.
  2. Upload master.wav and cover_3000x3000.jpg, then copy each field from release_sheet.txt.
  3. Set the release date at least 2–3 weeks ahead, so you can pitch the song to playlists before it's out.
  4. Once the distributor assigns an ISRC and UPC, save them and reuse them for every future release of this recording.
  5. Upload the visualizer to YouTube, and use the promo loop as Spotify Canvas or as a Reels/Shorts background.
